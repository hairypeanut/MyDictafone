Recording and Transcription

MyDictafone requires microphone and speech recognition permission. These permissions can be reviewed in your iPhone or iPad Settings.

Speech recognition accuracy can vary depending on background noise, pronunciation, microphone quality and the selected speaking language.

Translation

Your device may need to download language resources from Apple before translation can begin.

If translation cannot be prepared, make sure your device has an internet connection and try again. Some language combinations may not currently be supported by Apple.

Saved Transcripts

MyDictafone saves transcripts as text files on your device. Saved transcripts can be accessed through MyDictafone's Recent and Files sections.

Sharing

Transcripts can be shared as raw text or as text files using the standard iOS and iPadOS Share Sheet.

Privacy

MyDictafone is designed around local transcript storage and does not operate a developer-controlled server for storing your dictations.
