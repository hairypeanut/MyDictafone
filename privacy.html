MyDictafone Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MyDictafone is designed to provide a simple and private way to record, transcribe, save, translate and share your spoken words.

Information We Collect

MyDictafone does not operate a developer-controlled server for storing your dictations or transcripts. We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDKs or Firebase to store your transcript content.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

MyDictafone requires access to your device's microphone and Apple's speech recognition technologies to convert spoken words into text.

Depending on the language, device configuration and Apple's services, speech recognition may require network access and information may be processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's privacy policies.

Saved Transcripts

Saved transcripts are stored as text files on your device using the storage facilities provided by iOS and iPadOS. MyDictafone uses Apple's Data Protection capabilities for files created by the app.

You are responsible for managing, deleting, exporting and backing up your saved transcript files.

Translation

MyDictafone uses Apple's Translation technologies to provide transcript translation. Your device may need to download language resources from Apple before translation can begin.

Availability of languages and language combinations is determined by Apple and may vary by device, operating system and region.

Sign in with Apple

Where Sign in with Apple is used, authentication is handled by Apple. MyDictafone does not receive your Apple ID password.

Face ID, Touch ID and Device Authentication

Where device authentication is enabled, MyDictafone may use Apple's LocalAuthentication system to protect access to the app.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode verification are handled by iOS or iPadOS. MyDictafone does not receive or store your biometric information.

Sharing and Exporting

MyDictafone allows you to share or export text using Apple's system sharing features. When you choose to share content with another app, service or person, that destination may have its own privacy practices.

MyDictafone does not automatically share your transcripts.

Advertising and Tracking

MyDictafone does not use your transcripts for advertising and does not include third-party advertising or behavioural tracking technology.

Your Choices

You control what you record, save, translate, export and delete. Microphone, speech recognition and other permissions can be managed through your device's Settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as MyDictafone develops or as platform requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
